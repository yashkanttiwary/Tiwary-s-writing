The whole world was on my lips for the whole twenty minutes of it.  
So I hugged her tight and I laughed within,  
wondering if I consumed the whole of it,  
what joy it would be, and what it would make of me.  

She was actually sweet. Not just the talk of it.  
She was everything, and she was real.  

I laughed at myself now, thinking of it.  
I laughed at myself.  
How I thought I could consume all of it.  
And I cry at why I cannot do it.
